Welcome to Aba City

Aba is a major city in southeastern Nigeria and serves as the commercial nerve center of Abia State. It is located on the west bank of the Aba River and is a key urban settlement for the Igbo people, particularly the indigenous Ngwa clan . The city is often nicknamed "Enyimba," which means "the Great Bull," signifying its status as a chief city

Aba Nigeria Temple

The Aba Nigeria Temple is a prominent religious landmark and the 121st operating temple of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church) . It holds immense historical and spiritual significance as the first Latter-day Saint temple built in Nigeria, the second in West Africa, and the third on the African continent . Dedicated in 2005, it serves Latter-day Saints not only from across Nigeria but also from Cameroon
