Wild Waters Rafting

Our Adventure Story

Wild Waters Rafting sort of captures that wild side of rivers, you know, the rush and all the natural stuff around it, plus hanging out with people on these trips. I think every quick drop in the water has its own little tale, and the whole ride just pulls everyone closer in some way. They have these guides who really know what they are doing, gear that is solid, and they are all about getting outside, so it ends up feeling safe but still pretty intense, kind of wild like the name says.

History

It started back in 2010, this company, with just a couple of people who loved rivers starting out with one raft, wanting to let others try the excitement of going down whitewater. Those early weekend runs on the South Fork turned into something bigger pretty fast, like a real business. They kept adding more rafts over time, got their guides trained up on safety and skills, built this name for good times out there. And they stick to caring about the environment and helping out locally, that is still what drives them.

Now they run trips on different rivers in the area, stuff for newbies or people who have done it a ton. The whole thing comes from all those happy moments, laughs with the group, and this idea that anyone can get into the river scene. They keep changing things up a bit but hold onto that original free spirit from the start. Maybe join in and see what happens next, one twist after another.
