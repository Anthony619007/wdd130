About All-Outdoors

Four generations of river enthusiasts. We’ve been sharing the thrill of California whitewater since 1972. What started as a handful of friends with wooden oars is now a premier outfitter with permits on the most legendary rivers.

Owners & guides

Brothers Mark and Jenna Rivera took over from their parents in 2005. Every guide is swift-water certified, wilderness first responder trained, and genuinely loves the river. We keep ratios low (max 6 per guide).

Our signature adventures

Class III+

South Fork American

Half day · 10mi · Chili Bar to Salmon Falls. Perfect for first-timers and families. Includes wetsuit & splash jacket.

Equipment: Self-bailing rafts, NRS paddles, helmets, PFDs.

Class IV+

Tuolumne (Cherry Creek)

Full day · expert · 18mi continuous whitewater. Technical boulder gardens & big drops.

Equipment: High-side rafts, premium self-bailers, carbon fiber paddles, drysuites available.

Class III-IV

Merced River Canyon

2 days / 1 night • wilderness camping. Pristine granite & big rapids.

Equipment: Oar/paddle rafts, full camping gear, kitchen setup, throw bags, and safety kayak.
