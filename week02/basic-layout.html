Houses of the Lord: Latter-day Temples

The Purpose of Temples

Temples are sacred Spaces where members of The church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints draw closer to heavenly father, make Sacred covenants, and participate in ordinances that unite families for eternity. They are literally houses of rhe Lord-places of peace, revelation, and Spiritual refuge from the world.

Temples Around the World

From the majestic Salt Lake Temple in Utah to the stunning Rome Italy Temple, over 300 temples now dot the earth-with many more announced and under construction. Each temple stands as a beacon of faith, hope, and divine love, welcoming all who seek spiritual strength and eternal connections.

"Holiness to the Lord: The House of the Lord. " - Inscription on every Latter-day saint temple
