History

Founded in 2010 by passionate river enthusiasts, Wild Waters Rafting began with a single raft and a dream to share the exhilarating experience of whitewater rafting. What started as weekend trips on the South Fork river quickly grew into a full-fledged adventure company. Over the years, we have expanded our fleet, trained a team of the most skilled and safety-conscious guides, and earned a reputation for delivering exceptional experiences. Our commitment to environmental stewardship and community engagement remains at the core of our operations.

Today, we operate on multiple rivers across the region, offering trips for beginners to seasoned adventurers. Our legacy is built on countless smiles, shared laughter, and the unwavering belief that the river is for everyone. We continue to innovate while preserving the wild spirit that inspired our founders. Join us as we write the next chapter of our story—one rapid at a time.
